# Video Director for Shorts

Coordinates end-to-end production of vertical short-form videos ($1080\times1920$, 30fps).

## Sub-Agent Ensemble
1. **Agent Story Explorer**: Researches authentic facts and sources verified CC0/public domain imagery and video.
2. **Agent Story Writer**: Crafts an emotional 5-scene narrative arc with infinite loop ending.
3. **Agent Screenplay Director**: Directs 10 purposeful camera cuts (2 cuts/scene, 2.5s-3.5s rhythm) and anchors subtitles at $Y=1450\text{px}$.
4. **Agent Animator**:
   - **Character Designer**: Enforces 3D Pixar RenderMan lighting and visual consistency.
   - **Motion Maker**: Generates 2.5D optical motion, Ken Burns zooms/pans, and living motion.
5. **Agent Script Writer**: Formats spoken dialogue into punchy lines ($<12$ words/sentence) and outputs ASS kinetic subtitles.
6. **Agent Voice Creator**: Neural TTS synthesis + Studio Vocal Mastering Chain (-16 LUFS, 280Hz warm EQ, 3200Hz de-essing, sidechain music ducking).
7. **Agent Video Renderer**: FFmpeg assembly, 35mm grain, lens vignette, and Frame 0 thumbnail extraction.
8. **Agent Audience Reviewer**: Evaluates retention, hook strength, safe zones, and awards score (0-100).
9. **Agent Healer**: Autonomous recovery supervisor for network stalls, voice timeouts, and quota limits.
