# Cartoon Shorts Maker

Create captivating 3D Pixar-style animated story shorts for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels completely free.

## Production Process

```
1. 4-BEAT STORY SCRIPT  ──▶ 2. SCENE IMAGE GENERATION (3D Cartoon Style)
                                        │
3. STORYTELLER VOICEOVER ──▶ 4. KEN BURNS CAMERA ANIMATION (Pan/Zoom)
                                        │
5. FINAL 1080x1920 MP4   ◀── 6. DUCKED MUSIC & KINETIC CAPTIONS
```

## The 4-Beat Story Structure (~20-30s)

1. **Beat 1: The Ordinary World & Unique Character**
   - Introduce an adorable or unlikely character with a distinct visual trait (e.g. Pip the tiny rusty robot with glowing blue eyes).
2. **Beat 2: The Spark of Wonder / Discovery**
   - The character uncovers an unexpected or magical artifact (e.g. a glowing green sprout beneath gears).
3. **Beat 3: The Climax / Action**
   - The character takes bold action against the odds (e.g. carrying the seed to the highest mountain and watering it).
4. **Beat 4: The Bloom & Infinite Loop**
   - A breathtaking visual transformation (e.g. a massive sunflower brings life to the world), ending on an inspiring payoff that loops back to the start.

## Audio & Voice Guidelines

- **Narrator Voice:** `en-US-JennyNeural` (warm, storybook wonder) or `en-GB-RyanNeural` (thoughtful, classic tale).
- **Music Bed:** Harmonic major chords (G-major, C-major, chimes) gently ducked to 20% volume beneath speech.
- **Captions:** Yellow primary color (`#00FFFF`), thick outline (`Outline=6`), bold typography, centered in mobile safe zone.
