# 🤖 Pip Story Journey Engine

When the user requests to make a new video with Pip (e.g., *"create a new video with Pip"*, *"what does Pip find next?"*, *"make another Pip journey"*), this engine activates to brainstorm an original story, generate visuals adhering to Pip's character bible, voice with human warmth, and render a complete 1080×1920 MP4 Short.

---

## 1. Character Integrity & Visual Contract

All scenes MUST adhere to the official [CHARACTER_BIBLE.md](file:///Users/sumanth/Downloads/New/characters/pip/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md):
- **Subject:** Pip (petite, weathered vintage copper and brass robot, large glowing circular blue ocular eyes, curled spring antenna, glowing amber/emerald chest core).
- **Style:** 3D Pixar Disney feature animation aesthetic (RenderMan look, rich textures, soft rim lighting, tactile metal/glass surfaces).
- **Tone:** Silent, empathetic, curious hero discovering hidden wonder in a desolate rust world.
- **Narrator:** Omniscient storyteller using **`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`** (+3% rate) with warm human inflection and breathing pauses.

## 2. Pacing & Pacing Blueprint (20–45s)

| Beat | Timestamp | Story Beat | Visual & Camera Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Hook** | 0.0s – 3.5s | The Impossible Discovery | Frame 0 curiosity. Wide shot zooming in on Pip stumbling upon a glowing mystery in the scrap. |
| **2. Contrast** | 3.5s – 7.0s | The Skepticism / Threat | Close punch-in on Pip's glowing eyes protecting the fragile object against harsh elements or grumpy machines. |
| **3. Devotion** | 7.0s – 12.0s | The Selfless Act | Macro focus on Pip offering his battery, coolant, or body to shelter/nurture the miracle. |
| **4. Miracle** | 12.0s – 18.0s | Colossal Payoff | Spectacular visual eruption: vibrant light, blooming color, transformed landscape. |
| **5. Loop** | 18.0s – 24.0s | Innocent Joy & Reset | Pip leaps or smiles in joy; philosophical reflection loops directly back to the opening hook phrase. |
