## 💡 Upcoming Journey Idea Bank (Story Backlog)

Whenever the user asks to create a new video with Pip, the Pip Story Director agent can draw from or expand upon these concepts:

### Journey 02: *Pip & The Fallen Star*
- **Logline:** Pip finds a shivering baby star buried under cold steel and builds a clockwork catapult, giving his own chest battery to launch it back to the constellation.
- **Visuals:** Cold jagged iron ruins ➔ glowing warm starlight ➔ celestial explosion across the galaxy.
- **Emotional Hook:** "Some things are too bright to stay on the ground."

### Journey 03: *Pip & The Clockwork Firefly*
- **Logline:** In an abandoned clock tower, Pip discovers a mechanical brass firefly with a bent wing. He carefully replaces the wing with an antique pocket watch gear, waking thousands of dormant fireflies that illuminate the entire dark metropolis.
- **Visuals:** Dusty Victorian clockwork interior ➔ intricate miniature repair ➔ golden glowing swarm swirling in the night sky.

### Journey 04: *Pip & The Singing Water Crystal*
- **Logline:** Pip discovers a crystal prism that produces musical frequencies whenever a single raindrop hits it. When he cleans the prism and aligns it with the rising sun, the refraction creates an aurora of liquid sound that melts the rust off ancient metal sculptures.
- **Visuals:** Macro rain droplets ➔ crystalline light refractions ➔ shimmering rainbow auroras over foggy scrap valleys.

### Journey 05: *Pip & The Giant's Heart*
- **Logline:** Pip stumbles upon a dormant, moss-covered colossal guardian robot half-buried in a ravine. Finding the giant's cracked glass core, Pip gently installs a glowing flower inside the heart chamber, awakening the gentle giant with a peaceful smile.
- **Visuals:** Massive rusted iron hand vs tiny Pip ➔ glowing flora heart installation ➔ giant gently picking Pip up to view the sunset together.
